A number that controls how strongly a signal affects a decision.
Active model
The version currently making decisions. Shown in gray.
Candidate
Your proposed version. Shown in green until you choose to use it.
Evaluate
Check the proposed version against examples with known answers.
Promote

Rollback
Bring back the original weights. The experiment history stays.
